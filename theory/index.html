Theory

Notes on head and neck anatomy, organised into 13 regions, from the skull and vertebral column to the ear and temporal bone. They are written for this site, and each section says which chapter or page of the textbooks it comes from, so you can check it and read further.

Regions are added one at a time. The table of contents shows what is covered so far.

How the notes are built

  • Every section ends with a sources line. Where the two textbooks differ, the notes say so.
  • Clinical boxes, marked in red, link the anatomy to what it means in practice.
  • Diagram links open the matching layers in the diagram viewer.
  • Points that come from the author's own teaching and dental knowledge, rather than the textbooks, are labelled "Author's notes".

Textbooks used

  1. Sinnatamby CS. Last’s Anatomy: Regional and Applied. 12th ed. Elsevier; 2011. Cited by chapter, part and heading, because the copy used has no page numbers.
  2. Koshi R, editor. Cunningham’s Manual of Practical Anatomy, head and neck volume. 16th ed. 2018. Cited by chapter and page.

The notes are an aid to study, not a substitute for the textbooks or for the course material.
